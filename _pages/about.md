About me
====
I'm a new Ph.D graduate student in astronomy at [The Ohio State University](https://www.osu.edu). I come from China and get a B.S. degree in Mathematics and Physics from [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/). Before entering university, I grew up and got my education in [Luanchuan County, Luoyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luanchuan_County), a beautiful place. My hometown was designated as [national-level poor county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National-level_poor_county) by the Chinese government before 2019. Through ["Ziqiang Program"](https://join-tsinghua.edu.cn/gnxs/zqjh.htm), I entered Tsinghua University in 2022 sucessfully.

I want to devote myself into astronomy. I have a broad interest in different fields of astronomy, from exoplanet to cosmology, from theory to observation. In detail, I have related research experience in microlensing(theory and observation, supervised by Prof. [Shude Mao](https://en.westlake.edu.cn/faculty/Shude-Mao.html)) as well as weak lensing(theory, supervised by Prof. [Henk Hoekstra](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/staffmembers/henk-hoekstra)). I also worked on deep learning and combine it with the weak lensing work as the topic of my graduate thesis (supervised by Prof. [Yuan-sen Ting](https://www.google.com/search?client=safari&rls=en&q=Yuansen+Ting&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8)). For more details, see my CV section in header or download my new [CV](https://cloud.tsinghua.edu.cn/f/6f74d92b3bd0494092ba/?dl=1).

My first research project on microlensing has been [published](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/1538-3881/ae14f3) in AJ (received directly), revealing a 5~10% overestimation on microlensing planetary sensitivity caused by the ignorance of binary-lens degeneracy. This effect is necessary to be mitigated in next-generation [Earth 2.0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.06693) and [Roman](https://roman.gsfc.nasa.gov) mission. 

In daily life, I am really into the nature. Besides the starry sky we always study, I like forests, mountains and especially the sea. I am good at hiking, which is a skill I learned when I was a child in my mountain hometown. I am now fascinated by skiing and expecting next winter. After arriving in America, I felt interested in cooking and I can cook several Chinese cuisine very well, like Hot Oil-Splashed Noodles, Big Plate Chicken, and Cold Noodles with Sesame Sauce.

I consider the connection between people important. When in Tsinghua University, I devoted a lot of time into the social organization and scientific outreach by joining Tsinghua Astronomy Society(TAS). TAS is an interest-club in Tsinghua University, with more than 2000 members including undergraduates and graduates. I was the scientific minister during 2024-2025. The focus of this position is about popularization of science and scientific outreach.
